## Full description (max 4000)

Hisaab saaf. Dosti barkaraar.

Splitr makes sharing money with friends simple. Trips, flat rent, office lunches, weekend dinners, a wedding, a cricket night: add the bill, choose how to split it, and Splitr keeps track of who owes whom.

SPLIT ANY BILL YOUR WAY
• Equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares
• Several people can pay one bill
• Shares always add up to the exact amount, to the last paisa or cent
• Type "dinner 900 with Asha" and quick-add fills the form for you

SETTLE UP WITH FEWER PAYMENTS
• Smart settle-up works out who really needs to pay whom, so a long list of debts becomes a handful of payments
• Record payments when they happen
• In India, friends can pay you straight from their UPI app using the UPI ID you save
• Send a friendly reminder, including on WhatsApp

GROUPS AND FRIENDS
• Create groups for trips, home, work or anything else
• Invite people with a link or a QR code they scan with their phone camera
• Balances between friends outside any group
• Comments, emoji reactions and a full edit history on every expense

MADE FOR REAL LIFE
• Add expenses even without signal: they are saved on your phone and sent when you are back online, never twice
• Several currencies: each debt stays exact in its own currency, and your totals are shown in your home currency
• Recurring expenses for rent, Wi-Fi and subscriptions
• Spending insights and group budgets
• Import your history from Splitwise with a CSV export
• Light and dark mode and a choice of themes

YOURS
• Free, with no ads and no selling of your data
• Download all your data, or delete your account, from Settings
• Works on Android, on the web and (soon) on iPhone with the same account

Splitr does not move money. Payments happen in your own UPI or banking app, and you record them in Splitr.

Questions? Open Settings or visit https://www.splitr.pro/support
