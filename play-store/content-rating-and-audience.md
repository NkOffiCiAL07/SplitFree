# Content rating, target audience and app access

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC), answers from the app's real behaviour
- Category: **Utility, productivity, communication or other** (an expense-sharing tool).
- Violence: **No**. Sexuality / nudity: **No**. Profanity / crude humour: **No**. Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco): **No**.
- Gambling or simulated gambling: **No**. Real-money games or contests: **No**.
- User-generated content / user interaction: **Yes, limited.** People in the same group can see each other's expense descriptions, comments and reactions. There is no public feed, no public profiles and no chat with strangers.
- Shares the user's location with others: **No**. Allows purchases of digital goods: **No**.
- Expected result: about **Everyone / PEGI 3 / IARC 3+** (the "users can interact" note may add an "Users Interact" label).

## Target audience
- Target age groups: **18 and over** (choose "18+"). The product moves money between adults and requires a mobile number; it is not designed for children.
- "Appeals to children": **No**. Do not tick any under-13 age group, or the Families policy would apply.
- Privacy policy says the app is not directed at children under 13.

## App access
- Parts of the app need an account. In *App access* choose "All or some functionality is restricted" and give the reviewer **test credentials**. Create a dedicated reviewer account in production (email + password, a mobile number, a group with a few sample expenses) and paste it into Play Console. **Do not commit these credentials to Git.**

## Ads
- Contains ads: **No**.

## News app / COVID-19 / Government / Health: **No** to all.
